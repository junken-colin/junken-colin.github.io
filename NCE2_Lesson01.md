# Lesson 1 — A Private Conversation

## 一、课文原文（难点单词已高亮）

Last week I went to the **theatre**. I had a very good **seat**. The play was very **interesting**. I did not enjoy it. A young man and a young woman were **sitting** behind me. They were talking very **loudly**. I could not hear a word. I got very angry. I could not hear the actors. I turned round. I looked at the man and the woman **angrily**. They did not pay any **attention**. At last, I said, "I can't hear a word! You are **disturbing** me!" The young man said rudely, "This is a private **conversation**!"

## 三、简单句口语复述

> Last week I went to the theatre. I had a good seat. The play was interesting, but I didn't enjoy it. A young man and a young woman were sitting behind me, talking loudly. I couldn't hear the actors. I got angry and turned round. I told them to be quiet. But the young man said rudely, "This is a private conversation!"

---

## 四、扩展口语问答参考

**Q1: Have you ever been disturbed while watching a movie or a play?**

> A: Yes, once. I was watching a movie in the cinema and a group of people behind me kept talking and laughing. I felt really annoyed because I couldn't follow the story. In the end, I asked the staff to help.

**Q2: What do you usually do when someone disturbs you in public?**

> A: It depends. If it's not too bad, I just ignore it. But if it really bothers me, I'll turn around and politely ask them to keep quiet. I don't want to start an argument, but I also want to enjoy myself.

**Q3: Do you prefer watching a play at the theatre or watching a movie at the cinema? Why?**

> A: I prefer the cinema because it's more relaxing. The theatre is nice, but you have to dress up and sit quietly for a long time. At the cinema, I can just grab some popcorn and enjoy the show. Besides, the tickets are usually cheaper.
