Lesson 1 — A Private Conversation

新概念英语 第二册

课文原文

简单句口语复述

Last week I went to the theatre. I had a good seat. The play was interesting, but I didn't enjoy it. A young man and a young woman were sitting behind me, talking loudly. I couldn't hear the actors. I got angry and turned round. I told them to be quiet. But the young man said rudely, "This is a private conversation!"

扩展口语问答参考

Q1: Have you ever been disturbed while watching a movie or a play?

Yes, once. I was watching a movie in the cinema and a group of people behind me kept talking and laughing. I felt really annoyed because I couldn't follow the story. In the end, I asked the staff to help.

Q2: What do you usually do when someone disturbs you in public?

It depends. If it's not too bad, I just ignore it. But if it really bothers me, I'll turn around and politely ask them to keep quiet. I don't want to start an argument, but I also want to enjoy myself.

Q3: Do you prefer watching a play at the theatre or watching a movie at the cinema? Why?

I prefer the cinema because it's more relaxing. The theatre is nice, but you have to dress up and sit quietly for a long time. At the cinema, I can just grab some popcorn and enjoy the show. Besides, the tickets are usually cheaper.
